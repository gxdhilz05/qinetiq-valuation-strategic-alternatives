# Project checklist

## 1. Understand the company and the project question : What is Qinetiq worth on a standalone basis, and which strategic option for the US business would maximise shareholder value?

- [ ] Explain QinetiQ's products and services in plain English
- [ ] Identify its principal customers and geographies
- [ ] Understand EMEA Services and Global Solutions
- [ ] Explain the LTPA and other important contracts
- [ ] Map order intake, backlog, revenue, profit, and cash flow
- [ ] Summarise the US operating issues and strategic review
- [ ] Record all sources in `research/sources.csv`

## Initial Hypotheses
- EMEA is the higher quality core business.
- The US business may be depressing the group's valuation.
- Backlog provides revenue visibility but must convert into cash.
- A US sale might unlock value but only if the price is attractive.
  
## 2. Collect and clean historical data

- [ ] Income statement
- [ ] Balance sheet
- [ ] Cash-flow statement
- [ ] Segment revenue and operating profit
- [ ] Orders, backlog, margins, and cash conversion
- [ ] Underlying-to-statutory reconciliation

## 3. Build forecasts

- [ ] Forecast revenue and margins by segment
- [ ] Forecast taxes, D&A, capex, and working capital
- [ ] Calculate unlevered free cash flow
- [ ] Build base, upside, and downside cases

## 4. Value the company

- [ ] DCF
- [ ] Trading comparables
- [ ] Sum-of-the-parts valuation
- [ ] Enterprise-value-to-equity-value bridge
- [ ] Implied value per share and sensitivities

## 5. Evaluate strategic options

- [ ] Retain the US business
- [ ] Restructure the US business
- [ ] Divest the US business
- [ ] Recommend an option and define key risks

## 6. Communicate the answer

- [ ] Complete 8–10-slide presentation
- [ ] Export final model outputs
- [ ] Prepare 30-second and two-minute explanations
- [ ] Review every formula, source, and assumption
