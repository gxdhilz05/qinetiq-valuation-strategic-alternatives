# Forecast assumptions

## Model structure

The model will compare three outcomes for Global Solutions:

1. Retain with limited improvement.
2. Retain and restructure.
3. Divest the US business.

One forecast model will use a scenario selector. This avoids maintaining three separate Excel files.

The assumptions below are the initial base case. They assume both segments remain in the group, no acquisitions or disposals, and constant exchange rates.

## Formulas

```text
Revenue = Previous-year revenue x (1 + revenue growth)
Operating profit = Revenue x operating margin
Group revenue = EMEA revenue + Global Solutions revenue
Group operating profit = EMEA operating profit + Global Solutions operating profit
Group margin = Group operating profit / Group revenue
```

## Reasons for the assumptions

- **FY27 EMEA growth:** Strong funded backlog, the LTPA extension and current UK defence programmes support 5% growth.
- **FY27 Global growth:** The US business has stabilised, but its recovery is not yet proven, so revenue is held flat.
- **FY28-FY29:** EMEA continues converting contracted work. Global Solutions recovers gradually rather than immediately.
- **FY30-FY31:** Growth reduces as visibility falls and the forecast approaches a sustainable long-term rate.
- **EMEA margin:** Kept close to 12% because higher utilisation and efficiency are balanced by wages, investment and competitive procurement.
- **Global margin:** Gradually rises towards 10% as lower-return work is exited and the cost base improves.
- **AI and defence technology:** Autonomy, cyber, mission data and directed energy support demand, but no separate AI growth premium is added.
- **Inflation:** Revenue growth is nominal, so ordinary price increases are already included. Inflation is not added twice.
