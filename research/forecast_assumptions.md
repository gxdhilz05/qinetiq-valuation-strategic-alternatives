# Forecast assumptions

## Model structure

The model will compare three outcomes for Global Solutions:

1. Retain with limited improvement.
2. Retain and restructure.
3. Divest the US business.

One forecast model will use a scenario selector instead of three separate Excel files. The assumptions below are the initial base case. They assume both segments remain in the group, no acquisitions or disposals, and constant exchange rates.

## Formulas

```text
Revenue = Previous-year revenue x (1 + revenue growth)
Operating profit = Revenue x operating margin
Group revenue = EMEA revenue + Global Solutions revenue
Group operating profit = EMEA operating profit + Global Solutions operating profit
Group margin = Group operating profit / Group revenue
```

## Forecast approach

FY27 is anchored to management's guidance of 3-5% group revenue growth and an 11.0-11.5% underlying operating margin. The segment assumptions produce approximately 4.0% growth and an 11.2% margin, both near the middle of those ranges.

The later years rely less on management guidance because visibility decreases. Growth is gradually reduced towards a sustainable rate rather than assuming that current defence investment and backlog growth continue indefinitely.

## Revenue assumptions

### EMEA Services

- **FY27: 5.0%.** FY26 organic growth was approximately 4%. The £4.1bn funded backlog, LTPA extension to 2033 and current UK defence programmes support a modest increase. The assumption remains below double-digit growth because government budgets can take time to become delivered contracts.
- **FY28: 5.0%.** LTPA investment, Typhoon engineering work, Defence Digital programmes and demand for test and evaluation should continue converting into revenue.
- **FY29: 4.5%.** Contracted work still provides support, but some of the strongest backlog conversion will already have occurred.
- **FY30: 4.0%.** Visibility becomes weaker and procurement timing, competition and customer affordability become more important.
- **FY31: 3.5%.** Growth approaches a sustainable nominal rate for a mature government-services business.

### Global Solutions

- **FY27: 0.0%.** The US operation has stabilised after restructuring, but FY26 organic revenue declined and the recovery has not yet been demonstrated. Holding revenue flat avoids assuming an immediate turnaround.
- **FY28: 2.0%.** A smaller and more focused cost base should allow modest growth if contract execution improves.
- **FY29-FY31: 3.0%.** This assumes gradual recovery in areas such as space, missile defence, surveillance, sensing and autonomy. It remains conservative compared with the stronger growth recorded before the recent US problems.

## Margin assumptions

### EMEA Services

The margin stays close to 12%. Higher facility utilisation, scale and digital efficiency could help profitability. These benefits are balanced by specialist wage costs, continued technology investment and competitive government procurement. The forecast therefore allows only a small increase rather than a large margin expansion.

### Global Solutions

The margin begins at 8.5%, slightly below FY26's 9.0%, because contract and execution uncertainty remains. It then rises gradually to 10.0% as lower-return work leaves the portfolio, the reduced cost base takes effect and the contract mix improves. The forecast remains below the approximately 10.5% margins achieved before the recent problems.

## Commercial factors

- **Defence spending:** Higher UK and allied defence budgets support demand, but spending only becomes QinetiQ revenue after contracts are awarded and work is delivered.
- **Backlog:** The large EMEA backlog gives stronger near-term visibility. Global Solutions has much less funded backlog, which supports using lower growth assumptions.
- **AI and defence technology:** Autonomy, cyber, mission data, synthetic training and directed energy increase demand for testing and systems integration. No separate AI premium is added because the size and timing of the revenue benefit are uncertain.
- **Inflation:** The growth assumptions are nominal, so normal price increases are already included. Inflation may also raise wages and facility costs, limiting margin expansion.
- **Foreign exchange:** The base case assumes constant exchange rates. GBP/USD movements will be considered separately rather than hidden inside operating growth.
- **US strategic review:** This forecast assumes Global Solutions remains in the group. The retain, restructure and divest cases will change the US growth, margin, restructuring-cost and disposal assumptions.
