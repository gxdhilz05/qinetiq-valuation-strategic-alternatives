# QinetiQ Valuation and Strategic Alternatives Analysis

Independent investment-banking project analysing QinetiQ Group plc, its intrinsic valuation, and the strategic options available for its US business.

## Central question

What is QinetiQ worth, and should management retain, restructure, or divest its US operation?

## Planned outputs

- Five-year historical and forecast financial model
- DCF, trading-comparables, and sum-of-the-parts valuations
- Base, upside, and downside cases
- Assessment of the US strategic alternatives
- 8–10-slide management presentation
- Two-minute verbal recommendation

## Repository structure

```text
research/       Company research, commercial awareness, and source log
data/           Manually collected public financial and operating data
model/          Excel valuation model and supporting notes
presentation/   Final pitchbook and exported PDF
images/         Selected model outputs used in the presentation
```

## Current status

Step 1 — company background research and source collection.

## Data policy

All analysis will use publicly available information, principally company annual reports, results presentations, regulatory announcements, and government or industry sources. Every material figure and assumption will be traceable through the source log.
